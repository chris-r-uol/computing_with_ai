# Data

This directory holds every dataset used by the module. Do not edit this file by
hand: it is generated from `src/cwai/registry.py` by
`uv run python scripts/check_licences.py --write-readme`, and CI fails if the two
disagree.

## Why licence hygiene matters

Transport research runs on other people's data: police collision records,
government traffic counts, crowd-sourced maps. Each comes with conditions. Most
require you to credit the source in exactly the words the owner specifies, and
the OpenStreetMap licence also requires that anything you build from it stays
open under the same terms. Breaking those conditions can invalidate your right
to use the data at all, and a published result you cannot lawfully share cannot
be reproduced.

So before data enters this repository it is recorded in the registry with its
source, licence and attribution, and `scripts/check_licences.py` checks that
every file here is accounted for, that the licence texts are present in
`LICENSES/`, and that the total stays under 80 MB. Make the same habit part
of your own projects: record where data came from and what you may do with it
the day you download it, not the day you publish.

## Datasets

### STATS19 casualties

One row per person injured in a West Yorkshire collision, giving their age, sex, road-user type and injury severity; link it to collisions by collision reference.

- File: `stats19/casualties.parquet`
- Licence: OGL-v3.0
- Source: https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/cb7ae6f0-4be6-4935-9277-47e5ce24a11f/road-accidents-safety-data
- Attribution: Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

### STATS19 road collisions

One row per injury road collision reported to the police in West Yorkshire, with its date, time, location and severity.

- File: `stats19/collisions.parquet`
- Licence: OGL-v3.0
- Source: https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/cb7ae6f0-4be6-4935-9277-47e5ce24a11f/road-accidents-safety-data
- Attribution: Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

### Chengdu road network (OpenStreetMap)

The drivable roads of Chengdu, China, in the same format as the Leeds network so you can compare two very different cities.

- File: `osm/chengdu.gpkg`
- Licence: ODbL-1.0
- Source: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL).

### Leeds road network (OpenStreetMap)

The drivable roads of Leeds as a map file of junctions and road segments, ready to analyse as a graph.

- File: `osm/leeds.gpkg`
- Licence: ODbL-1.0
- Source: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL).

### Synthetic roadside sensor readings (clean)

Made-up but realistic traffic sensor readings over time with nothing wrong with them, to use as a baseline.

- File: `synthetic/sensor-clean.parquet`
- Licence: generated
- Source: scripts/make_synthetic.py
- Attribution: Generated for this module by scripts/make_synthetic.py; no third-party rights.

### Synthetic roadside sensor readings (faulty)

The same made-up sensor readings with faults such as stuck values and dropouts added, for practising anomaly detection.

- File: `synthetic/sensor-faulty.parquet`
- Licence: generated
- Source: scripts/make_synthetic.py
- Attribution: Generated for this module by scripts/make_synthetic.py; no third-party rights.

### DfT annual average daily flow (AADF) traffic counts

Yearly estimates of how many vehicles of each type use each counted stretch of road on an average day, published by the Department for Transport.

- File: `dft-counts/aadf.parquet`
- Licence: OGL-v3.0
- Source: https://roadtraffic.dft.gov.uk/downloads
- Attribution: Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

### STATS19 vehicles

One row per vehicle involved in a West Yorkshire collision, with its type, manoeuvre and driver details; link it to collisions by collision reference.

- File: `stats19/vehicles.parquet`
- Licence: OGL-v3.0
- Source: https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/cb7ae6f0-4be6-4935-9277-47e5ce24a11f/road-accidents-safety-data
- Attribution: Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
